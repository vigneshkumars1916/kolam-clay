Kolam & Clay ("we", "us") runs this online store. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it and the choices you have. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules.

What we collect

  • Order and booking details: your name, email address, phone number, billing and delivery address, the items or workshop seats you buy, and any gift note you add.
  • Payment details: payments are processed by our payment providers (such as Razorpay) and Shopify. We never see or store your full card number, UPI PIN or banking passwords.
  • Website usage: basic device and browsing information collected through cookies, such as pages viewed, items added to cart and your chosen display currency.
  • Messages: anything you send us by email, WhatsApp or our contact form.
  • Newsletter: your email address, only if you sign up.

How we use it

  • To process, pack, ship and support your order or workshop booking.
  • To send order confirmations, shipping updates and booking reminders.
  • To answer your questions and handle returns or complaints.
  • To send our newsletter, if you have opted in. You can unsubscribe at any time from any email.
  • To keep the store secure, prevent fraud and meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share only what is needed with:

  • Shopify, which hosts our store and checkout;
  • payment providers, to process your payment;
  • courier partners, to deliver your parcel;
  • authorities, when the law requires it.

Some of these providers may store data outside India, under their own security and privacy safeguards.

Cookies

We use cookies that are needed for the cart and checkout to work, and cookies that help us understand how the store is used. You can manage cookies in your browser and through the cookie banner where it is shown. Your display currency choice is stored only in your own browser.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires (generally 8 years). Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe. Other data is deleted when it is no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, update or delete your personal data, withdraw consent for marketing, or nominate someone to exercise these rights for you. Write to hello@kolamandclay.in and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact our Grievance Officer (details in our Contact information) or the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

Our store is not intended for children under 18. Workshop bookings for participants under 18 must be made by a parent or guardian.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
