Every Kolam & Clay piece is made by hand in our Chennai studio and packed by hand in paper and corrugated card, with no plastic. This policy explains where we ship, what it costs and how long it takes.

Dispatch

Orders placed before 2 pm IST on a working day (Monday to Saturday) leave the studio the next working day. Orders placed after 2 pm, on Sundays or on public holidays are dispatched one working day later. You will receive a tracking link by email as soon as your parcel ships.

Rates and delivery times

The exact rate for your address is shown at checkout before you pay. Delivery times are estimates from our couriers and can be longer during festivals, monsoon disruption or customs checks.

International orders

International orders are charged in Indian rupees (INR). Prices shown in other currencies on our website are converted for your reference only; your bank or card provider sets the final exchange rate. Import duties, taxes and customs fees charged by the destination country are not included and are paid by the receiver on delivery. If a parcel is refused or returned because duties were not paid, we will refund the order value minus the shipping cost.

Workshop tickets

Workshop tickets are not shipped. Your booking confirmation email contains the studio address, date and time of your session.

Damaged or lost parcels

If a piece arrives broken, email a photo of the piece and the outer box to hello@kolamandclay.in within 48 hours of delivery. We will send a replacement or a full refund, and you do not need to return the broken piece. If your tracking has not updated for 10 working days, write to us and we will trace the parcel with the courier and, if it is lost, replace or refund it.

Wrong address

Please check your delivery address before you pay. If you notice a mistake, email us before your order is dispatched and we will correct it. Parcels returned to us because of an incorrect address can be re-sent at the shipping rate above.
