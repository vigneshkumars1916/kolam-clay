We want you to love what you bring home. If something is not right, this policy explains how returns, exchanges, refunds and workshop changes work.

Returns

You can return unused pieces in their original packaging within 7 days of delivery. To start a return, email hello@kolamandclay.in with your order number and the pieces you would like to return.

  • Within India: we arrange a free courier pickup from your address.
  • International orders: return shipping is paid by the buyer, and we will share the address and packing guidance. Please use a tracked service.

Items that cannot be returned

  • One-of-a-kind pieces, which are made only once and marked as such on the product page.
  • Gift sets that include a personal gift note.
  • Pieces that have been used, washed or damaged after delivery.

Natural variations in size, colour, glaze flow and pattern are part of handmade pottery and are not treated as defects.

Damaged on arrival

If a piece arrives broken or with a manufacturing fault, email a photo of the piece and the outer box within 48 hours of delivery. We will send a replacement or a full refund, including shipping. You do not need to send the piece back. One-of-a-kind pieces that arrive broken are refunded in full, as they cannot be replaced.

Refunds

Once a returned piece reaches the studio and we have checked it, we refund the item price to your original payment method within 5–7 working days. Your bank may take a few more days to show the credit. Original shipping charges are refunded only if the piece was damaged or we made a mistake with your order.

Exchanges

To swap a piece for a different glaze or size, return it as above and place a new order. If you prefer, write to us and we will hold the new piece for you while the return is on its way.

Workshop bookings

  • You can move your seat to another available date up to 48 hours before your session at no cost.
  • You can cancel for a full refund up to 72 hours before your session.
  • Within 48 hours of the session we cannot refund or move the seat, but you are welcome to send a friend in your place. Just tell us their name.
  • If we have to cancel a session, we will offer you another date or a full refund.

Order cancellations

You can cancel an order for a full refund until it is dispatched. Email us as soon as possible with your order number.
